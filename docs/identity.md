# Lock Family identity: tokens

One page for anyone building a Lock Family site (lockfamily.dev today;
locksql.com, lockmq.com, lockfile.pro, lockhttp.com, leadeck.dev later).
The source of truth is [`assets/tokens.css`](../assets/tokens.css): link it
first, then your own stylesheet, and copy `assets/fonts/` next to it. Why
this direction: [design-note.md](design-note.md).

**The idea in one line: a printed ticket that a human signs.** Paper and
ink, ruled lines, a perforation, a stamp; the terminal stays a real dark
terminal, because `lox run` is one.

## Type

| Role | Family | Weights | Token | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Zilla Slab | 700 | `--font-display` | headings, the wordmark; never below 1.1rem |
| Text | Source Serif 4 | 400, 400 italic, 600 | `--font-text` | body copy, 1.0625rem / 1.6 |
| Mono | IBM Plex Mono | 400, 600 | `--font-mono` | code, commands, product names, form labels |

All three are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licences in `assets/fonts/OFL-*.txt`),
self-hosted as Latin-subset `woff2`, `font-display: swap`. Form labels (`.label`)
are mono 600, 0.75rem, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em, `--ink-2`.

## Colour

Two themes, each designed: **paper** (light) and **carbon** (dark, a warm
carbon copy, not navy). No gradients, no glows. Text tokens pass WCAG AA
(4.5:1) on `--paper`, `--paper-2` and `--card`; `tests/test_site.py`
checks it.

The status colours mirror `lox run`'s (lockhub `internal/loxrun/style.go`):
inside a terminal, use lox run's exact colours; on paper, use the tokens
above. Products have **no colour of their own**: a Lock is told apart by
its engraved glyph and its name, not by a hue.

## Space and shape

`--s-1` 4px · `--s-2` 8px · `--s-3` 12px · `--s-4` 16px · `--s-5` 24px ·
`--s-6` 32px · `--s-7` 48px · `--s-8` 72px. `--gutter` 16px (32px from 768px).
`--measure` 40rem for running text. `--radius` 3px: paper has corners, no
pills. Rules are 1px (`--rule`), a section opens on a 2px `--ink` rule with
its number (`01`, `02`…); a perforation is `--perf` (2px dashed `--rule`).
Lists of things are ledgers (rows between rules), not grids of cards.

## Logos

Generated by `python3 tools/logo_svg.py` (`--check` in the tests). One style
on a 128 grid: a 9px shackle, a 6px body outline, a 4.5px engraved glyph,
round caps and joins, `--ink` strokes on a `--card` fill; light and dark
inside the file (`prefers-color-scheme`), so it works as an `<img>`.

- `mark.svg`: the family, a padlock whose body is a ticket stub (notches,
  perforation, keyhole). Minimum 16px; clear space a quarter of its size.
- `locksql.svg`, `lockmq.svg`, `lockfile.svg`, `lockhttp.svg`: the same
  padlock, with a cylinder, a queue, a page or a globe engraved in its body.
- `lea.svg`: a sealed container with three agents, one waiting for you.

Do not recolour, outline, add a gradient or a shadow, or set a logo on a
background of the other theme. The wordmark is `lock family`, lowercase, in
the display face, next to the mark.

## Patterns

- **Ticket**: `--card` face, 1px `--ink` border, a 2px `--ink` rule under its
  head, fields as mono labels over text, a perforated stub with notches.
- **Stamp**: 3px double border in `--sign` (approved) or `--stamp` (denied),
  mono uppercase, rotated -3°. It animates in once; never under reduced motion.
- **Ledger**: rows with a time column and a who column, one rule between rows.
- **Terminal**: `--term-bg`, mono 0.82rem, real commands and real output only.
- **Tabs**: folder tabs on a sheet: square, mono, the selected one joins its panel.
- Motion: only what tells the flow (the stamp, the flow SVG); everything stops
  under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Lock sites and READMEs: the site kit

The Lock sites (locksql, lockmq, lockfile, lockhttp, locklog) and every
Lock README share one kit, kept in [`site-kit/`](../site-kit/KIT.md):

- `site-kit/assets/lock-site.css` and `copy.js`: the shared stylesheet and
  the install box's Copy button, identical bytes in every Lock repo.
- `site-kit/snippets/`: the head, header, hero, install box, family strip
  and footer of every page.
- `site-kit/tools/banner_svg.py`: the README banners (light and dark);
  `site-kit/tools/og.html`: the social card and touch icon.

The kit does not copy the tokens, fonts or logos: a Lock site takes
`assets/tokens.css`, `assets/fonts/` and `assets/<lock>.svg` from here.
Change the kit in this repo first, then copy it to the Lock repos.
